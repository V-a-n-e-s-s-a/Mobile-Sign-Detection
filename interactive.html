Think About the Scenario

You are walking through a building. Your smartphone camera sees a hallway with several signs. One sign is an EXIT sign, but it is far away and partially blocked by a person. The camera is also moving because you are walking.

What problems could the computer vision system have?

  • The sign may be too small in the image.
  • Part of the sign may be hidden.
  • Camera movement may cause motion blur.
  • Other signs and objects may create false detections.

This example shows why detecting signs in a real environment is more difficult than simply detecting a clearly visible sign in a test image.

What Did You Learn?

The main challenge with mobile sign detection is that signs do not always appear clearly in front of the camera.

A successful system needs to handle conditions such as distance, different viewpoints, occlusion, lighting, complex backgrounds, and camera movement. It also needs to consider the limited processing power of mobile devices and the amount of information that should be given to the user. [3] [4] [5] [6]
