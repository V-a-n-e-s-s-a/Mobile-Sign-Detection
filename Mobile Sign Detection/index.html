Welcome to the Tutorial

Listen to a short introduction before exploring the rest of the tutorial.

What if the sign is there—but the camera can't find it?

A sign may be small, far away, partially blocked, or difficult to see from the user's viewpoint. For someone navigating an unfamiliar environment, finding that sign can be an important part of knowing where to go.

01 · RESEARCH QUESTION

How can computer vision detect and locate signs from a mobile camera in difficult real-world conditions?

This tutorial focuses on the challenge of finding signs rather than simply reading their text. Real environments can make sign detection difficult because of distance, different viewpoints, occlusion, lighting, camera movement, and complex backgrounds.

02 · THE BASIC PIPELINE

From Camera to Information

A mobile sign-reading system can contain several steps. First, the camera captures the environment. The system then finds a possible sign and determines where it is. Reading the sign can happen after the sign has been detected.
